## 1. Information we collect

**You provide directly:**
- **Account details** — name, email, phone number, password (stored only as a
  secure hash, never in plain text), and account role (owner or tenant).
- **Profile details** — optional avatar image and bio.
- **Listing content (owners)** — property title, address, city, area, PIN code,
  rent, amenities, and property photos.
- **Leads/enquiries (tenants)** — when you contact an owner (call, WhatsApp,
  callback request), we record that enquiry and share your name and phone with
  that owner so they can respond.

**Collected automatically / with permission:**
- **Approximate & precise location** — only when you use "near me" search, to
  show listings around you. Used at request time; we do not track you in the
  background.
- **Camera / photo library** — only to let owners attach property photos or set
  an avatar. We access these only when you choose a photo.
- **Basic technical/log data** — error logs needed to keep the service working.

We do **not** knowingly collect any sensitive personal data.

## 2. How we use your information
- Create and manage your account and authenticate you.
- Show, search, and rank listings (including near your location).
- Connect tenants with owners for genuine rental enquiries.
- Process subscription payments for owners.
- Prevent fraud, abuse, and fake listings; enforce our Terms.
- Provide support and improve the app.

## 3. Payments
Owner subscription payments are processed by **Razorpay**. Payment card/UPI
details are entered on Razorpay's secure checkout and are handled by Razorpay —
we do **not** store your full card or UPI credentials. We keep a record of the
plan, amount, status, and Razorpay reference IDs. See Razorpay's privacy policy
at https://razorpay.com/privacy/.

## 4. How we share information
We do **not** sell your personal data. We share only:
- **Owner ⇄ tenant contact** for genuine enquiries, as described above.
- **Service providers** who help us operate (e.g. cloud hosting/database,
  Razorpay for payments), bound to protect your data.
- **Legal reasons** — if required by law or to protect rights and safety.

## 5. Data retention & deletion
We keep your data while your account is active. **You can delete your account at
any time** in the app: **Profile → Delete account**. This permanently removes
your account, your listings, your leads, and your payment history from our
active systems. You may also request deletion by emailing [privacy@yourdomain.com].
Some records may be retained where required by law (e.g. tax/accounting).

## 6. Security
Passwords are hashed (bcrypt). Access to backend systems is restricted. No
system is 100% secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your data.

## 7. Children
Roomzy is not directed to children under 18 and we do not knowingly collect
their data.

## 8. Your rights
Subject to applicable law (including India's DPDP Act), you may access, correct,
or delete your data, and withdraw consent. Contact [privacy@yourdomain.com].
